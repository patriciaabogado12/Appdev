# Favorite K-Drama Series: Youth of May

*Youth of May* is a poignant historical melodrama written by Lee Kang. Set against the backdrop of the May 1980 Gwangju Uprising, the story follows a passionate medical student and a resilient nurse whose lives become deeply intertwined amidst political turmoil.

"Even if we get separated, let's meet again in May." — Hwang Hee-tae


## Why It Stands Out
* *Heart-wrenching historical setting* capturing the humanity and tragedy of the 1980 Gwangju Uprising
* *Authentic retro atmosphere* with nostalgic 1980s fashion, music, and cinematography
* *Unforgettable chemistry* between the leads that brings raw emotion to every scene

## Main Characters
1. *Hwang Hee-tae* — A bright, free-spirited medical student who enters Seoul National University at the top of his class
2. *Kim Myung-hee* — A hardworking, independent nurse who stands firm against injustice and supports her family
3. *Lee Soo-chan* — A compassionate businessman helping his father run a pharmaceutical company
